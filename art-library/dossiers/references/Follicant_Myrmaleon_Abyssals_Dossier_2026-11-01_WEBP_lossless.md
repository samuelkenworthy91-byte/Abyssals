Creative identity
The juvenile uses an ant’s compact segmentation, leaf-tipped antennae, soft cream collar and nimble scissor mandibles. Evolution invokes the old bestiary antlion: a hulking, low-set insect predator with hooked jaws, stone-like chitin, leaf-shaped dorsal fins and a restrained, recognisably lion-like ruff. The sandy sinkhole is the action motif, not a separate body part.

Design logic: an early, moderately quick physical ant becomes a high-HP, high-Defence bruiser with a 120 Attack stat but merely 45 Speed. The 515 final BST matches established non-legendary examples, including Searwaste (515), Gourbog (515) and Cisternel (515). It exceeds Bastimole (470) but does not approach exceptional legendary Tellurion (580). These are audited baseline DATA species totals; Work should compare final runtime adjustments before merging.
Trade-offs: very poor special offence, exploitable Special Defence, low initiative, and Grass/Ground vulnerability to Ice (4×), Flying, Bug and Fire under conventional type rules. Its Grass resistance to Ground/Water and Ground immunity to Electric should be verified against Abyssals’ actual matchup table. Resistances are not a substitute for balancing the potential contact-trigger ability.

Pitfall Crown — NEW / PROPOSED
Trigger: when a living Myrmaleon with this ability receives actual direct HP damage from an opposing contact move, resolve the ordinary hit and contact reactions, then lower that attacker’s Speed by exactly 1 stage. At most once per distinct attacker UID per battle; its triggering set is battle-local and persists if Myrmaleon switches out and returns. A different opponent may trigger independently in doubles.
Restrictions: no effect if the move missed, failed, was blocked, dealt zero real HP damage, or only struck an effigy/substitute. Ignore indirect weather, poison, burn, recoil, delayed effects and self-damage. Do not trigger from allies or on already-fainted Myrmaleon. Respect existing stat-drop immunities, prevention abilities and −6 floor. If contact attacker fainted to another post-hit effect, do not apply a stage drop. No stacking with itself on subsequent hits from the same UID, including multi-hit moves.
Persistence: only during current battle, not the save record. Cleared on a new battle; retain across ordinary mid-battle switching, not between separate trainer encounters. No terrain prerequisite or extra damage. Suggested cue: a thin ring of sand collapses beneath the attacking foot/claw and a brief earthen crack punctuates the Speed fall, without replacing the move’s approved animation.
Balance note: no permanent binding, forced switching ban, free sandstorm, repeated Speed reductions or extra recoil; this is a defensive punishment, not a lockdown loop. If playtesting finds it weak against ranged opponents, prefer Ambush or Earthroot rather than silently buffing Pitfall Crown.

8  Breeding & inheritance
Use existing genderless, two-parent nursery rules. Follicant and Myrmaleon share one family ID. Two parents from this same line use the same-family inheritance union and the currently implemented independent Prodigy-stat rolls. Species choice remains the game’s existing base-form or either-parent rule; do not grant evolved hatchlings unless the live nursery explicitly supports them. Eggs hatch as Follicant for this family under ordinary breeding.
Preserve baseline chance ≈33.1759% per distinct offered Prodigy stat for same-family pairing (six-stat completion 1/750 when all are eligible), plus the currently supported Charm, Feed, Thread, Seal and Warmer logic. Inherited original growth bonuses compound with completed evolutions under the existing rules. Do not merge duplicate overlapping stats additively; stronger original successful donor bonus wins. No genders, new egg categories or special hatch times.
