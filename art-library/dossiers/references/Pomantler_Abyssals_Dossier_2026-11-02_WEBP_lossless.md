4. Abilities
Devil Orchard (NEW / PROPOSED): At the end of every third turn Pomantler remains active, one random horn-fruit ripens. Pomantler gains +1 in a random stat from Attack, Defense, Special Attack, Special Defense, or Speed. The trigger timer resets on switching out. Visual fruit colours may shift to indicate the current boon.
Alternative utility option: Natural Cure / Harvest-like fallback if Work prefers a simpler existing-style implementation.

6. Habitat and acquisition
Proposed location: sacred orchards, hidden forest shrines, or a late-midgame glade accessed after a side quest. Encounter around levels 36–42 as a rare overworld guardian or uncommon grove spawn.
7. Field Journal
Unseen rumour: Foresters speak of a stag whose antlers grow impossible fruits, each promising a different blessing to those it favours.
Caught entry: Pomantler's horn-fruits ripen with volatile magic. It is revered by herbalists, who believe each colour reflects a different mood of the forest itself.
8. Breeding
Single-stage genderless breeding line under the game's standard no-gender system. Egg species remains Pomantler.
