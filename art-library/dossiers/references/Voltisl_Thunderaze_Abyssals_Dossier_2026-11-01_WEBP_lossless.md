2. Core concept
A fast, special-attack focused Electric line in the mascot / companion space, but rendered in the Abyssals visual language rather than copying an existing franchise. The line is intentionally fragile: low bulk, explosive Speed and Special Attack, and an item-based evolution rather than a level-based one. The approved visuals are bright, energetic and highly readable, with Voltisl as the cute stage and Thunderaze as the sleek, more dramatic evolution.
3. Locked design notes
Keep both monsters pure Electric type.
Keep the approved battle sprites as the locked visual direction.
Voltisl should remain cheerful, small and cute, with oversized ears, cream chest fluff, yellow lightning markings and a zig-zag tail.
Thunderaze should remain recognisably the same species grown up: sharper face, longer fur, larger ears, more angular lightning motifs and visible electrical arcs.
Do not convert to Electric/Ground; retain the original pure Electric identity and original battle sprites.

Stat direction: both stages are fragile and should be easy to KO if caught, but they threaten teams immediately with Speed and Special Attack. Thunderaze gains a little extra HP and Sp.Def so it is not as paper-thin as Voltisl, while still staying firmly in glass-cannon territory.
6. Ability proposals (rough)
Primary concept: Static Body / Static Surge style effect — chance to paralyse on contact or small Electric power boost after being struck. Exact name can be settled later.
Alternative concept: a Speed-leaning Electric ability that rewards clean offensive play, but avoids making the line too snowbally.
Because this is a rough dossier stage, Work can compare against existing Abyssals abilities and choose the cleanest fit rather than introducing unnecessary new code.
7. Learnset direction
Keep the line heavily weighted toward special Electric damage and speed-oriented utility. Avoid pushing physical attack. Example move direction:
Early Voltisl: low-power Electric shots, quick utility, possibly a speed buff or evasive support move.
Mid / late Thunderaze: stronger special Electric attacks, one high-risk high-power nuke, and one or two utility options such as paralysis, pivoting or self-buffing.
Coverage should stay narrow enough that the family still feels defined by its Electric pressure rather than broad movepool sprawl.
8. Habitat and lore direction
Voltisl habitat: open meadows, storm-prone routes, outskirts of settlements and power-adjacent areas.
Thunderaze habitat: rarer storm zones, ridge routes, lightning-struck fields or elevated areas.
Field Journal tone: playful, energetic, and dangerously quick; crackling fur and markings store charge before explosive attacks.
9. Shiny guidance
Keep silhouettes and readability identical.
Potential shiny direction: cooler blue-white electricity with darker charcoal fur or, alternatively, pale silver fur with sharper gold accents.
Avoid palettes that weaken the clean Electric identity.
10. Work checklist
Use the included PNGs as the approved battle art references.
Create follower sheets, shiny variants and any additional battle presentation assets from these approved sprites.
Implement Thunder Shard as the evolution item if it does not already exist; otherwise map the family to the closest fitting existing item solution.
Place Thunder Shard on a random NPC house-gift interaction, per the design note.
Balance-test the final Speed and Sp.Atk so the line feels explosive but not oppressive.
