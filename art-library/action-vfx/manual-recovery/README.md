Abyssals — manual animation upload pack

1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Open https://github.com/samuelkenworthy91-byte/Abyssals/upload/main/art-library/action-vfx/manual-recovery
3. Upload the SIX PNGs inside Batch_01. Select the PNGs themselves, not the ZIP or Batch_01 folder. Keep their filenames unchanged.
4. Commit directly to main and tell ChatGPT the upload is finished. The sheets will then be verified by SHA-256, wired into the game on main, and tested before the remaining-animation audit.

Only six selected sheets need uploading: Nectarbane (replacement revision), Aura Pulse, Calm Thought, Mind Arc, Mind Lance and Mind Screen. The other 219 selected source sheets already exist byte-for-byte in the repository; their paths are listed in All_Selected_Sheets_Index.csv. Some are prepared source art awaiting installation. They do not need uploading again.

Every upload batch contains at most 50 PNG sheets; this pack requires just one batch of six. Filenames contain each sheet's own move ID and move name. Shared targets are recorded in the CSV/JSON, not duplicated into separate image files.

This pack contains the selected, prepared game sheets only. Rejected candidates, superseded revisions, review collages and raw generator images are excluded from upload. They remain available in the original full recovery ZIP. No new artwork was generated.

Recovery_Mapping.json preserves all 225 selected sources and 206 direct shared bindings (431 move/reaction bindings). This is a continuation record, not approval of unfinished live visual checks. Three of the eight planned additional source families still have no selected prepared sheet.
