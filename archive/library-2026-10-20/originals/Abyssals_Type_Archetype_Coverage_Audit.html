Moveset coverage changes

Before: 74/200 species had any damaging non-STAB move; 126 had none. Only 56/200 had any by level 16. After: 200/200 have coverage, and all 200 have a non-Normal damaging option by level 16 using their stronger attacking stat (Attack wins ties). Coverage means a damage-dealing move whose type is neither of that species’ own types; status moves and a dual species’ second STAB type do not count.

Physical Fairy options

Two existing shared collision moves were changed from Special to Physical, retaining their IDs, effects, PP, accuracy and authored animations. No new move IDs were added. The weaker option is learned at level 4 and the stronger at level 20 by physically inclined Fairy species where not already present; existing learnset entries keep their levels.

MovePowerAccuracyPPEffect

Physical/tied Fairy learners: Noctafey, Hareald, Leonarch, Oriflamme, Parlethorn, Caproclaim, Knuckluff. Existing saves refresh move definitions on load; eligible moves can be equipped through Aeric’s free relearning.

Coverage additions use limited themes from each species’ types, deterministic family preferences and moderate 40–70 power. Original learnset rows remain available, signature exclusivity remains intact, and no stats or typings were changed. Player-equipped four-move sets remain a player choice: access to coverage does not force it into every equipped set or guarantee a super-effective answer to every weakness.
