## Underworld transit
- Defeating Wardens unlocks permanent shortcuts.
- A central Underway Relay permits travel among cleared Circles.
- No carriage travel operates in Hell.
- Safe thresholds provide healing, reserve access, party management/save and possibly restricted essentials.

## Finale and restoration
- The escape from the finale is a short new Ascending Underworld route, not a full reverse traversal of all Nine Circles.
- Mortal settlements reuse the same physical maps with state changes such as occupation, recovery and restoration.
- Leader spare/execute results and optional content can alter restored-state NPCs, services and side content.
- Restoration cannot permanently remove any species from eventual obtainability.

## Primeval Reach postgame
Working structure:
- Botrune
- Tide Caves / expedition dock
- water crossing
- Primeval Reach
  - Fern Basin
  - Fossil Cliffs
  - Primeval Jungle
  - Caldera / nesting grounds

Purpose:
- living fossil populations
- alternate fossil hunting
- postgame completion access to the four final forms excluded by the current save's main-game story-branch choices
- those opposite story-branch forms are encountered/caught directly; Primeval Reach does not reverse the player's story choice or unlock the opposite evolution route for the source species
- no other postgame-exclusive first-access species without explicit revision

Story-branch completion set:
- Censmoke family: whichever of Thurafume / Malifume was not unlocked by the main-game relic choice
- Cairant family: whichever of Serapine / Pandemne was not unlocked by the main-game oath choice
- Flaggrim family: whichever of Oriflamme / Tattereign was not unlocked by the main-game banner choice
- Jerbune family: whichever of Concordune / Sundune was not unlocked by the main-game alliance choice
