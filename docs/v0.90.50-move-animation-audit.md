# Abyssals v0.90.50 — Move animation coverage and next art production pass

## Source audit
- 354 stable move IDs appear in `DATA.moves`.
- 63 pre-existing bespoke `ACTION_ART` sheets have individually keyed pixel sequences. Only **six of their move IDs** overlap the 354 entries in `DATA.moves`; the others cover additional move families outside that base list. The current 192px logical frames and 35%-longer timing remain unchanged.
- At the v0.90.50 baseline, **348 canonical moves** lack an individual sheet. The full game's move pool is larger than the 354-move base catalogue; auditing every runtime family requires a separate total. `MOVE_SCENES` has approximately 347 base scene score entries; an entry alone is not evidence of a unique sheet.
- Existing Mercy Edge/Mercy Ray source illustrations and the timing of Mercy's original scene must remain intact.
- Captures, double targets, leader portraits and perma-death may not be blocked by missing or slow animation assets.

## Required complete art-production milestone (not falsely marked complete in v0.90.50)

Make at least 348 additional original canonical 4×4, 16-frame pixel-action sheets in `dist/assets/vfx/action/` and register each unique move ID in `dist/action-vfx-data.js`, replacing only its fallback atlas rendering. Each animation should depict what the technique **does**, not simply its elemental typing: a Water cut should show blades and impacts, whereas a waterfall attack must communicate moving water; a physical fire punch has contact and punch follow-through rather than a generic flame burst. Distinguish anticipation, travel, contact, multi-hit cadence, recoil/drain, and recovery. Status moves need equally legible field or self effects. Preserve gameplay durations and existing mechanics.

Use a production queue of twelve sheets per check-in, prioritised by (a) most frequently used wild attacks, (b) party/leader techniques, (c) capture and status tools, (d) rare techniques. Save one sheet for one move, never a runtime random effect. Do not recolour the same art as a substitute for distinct action identity. All sheets target at least 192×192 logical pixels per frame and stay at the present visual screen size.

For each batch: assert coverage/unique keys, test generated frame integrity, simulate 0/1/2/3-hit outcomes where relevant, test reduced-motion, inspect mirrored action directions and phone viewports, and rerun leader-portrait no-freeze regressions. Rebuild signed APK only once batch assets and tests pass.
