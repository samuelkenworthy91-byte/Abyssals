# Clearer moves and leader battle fix — v0.90.49

Leader battles wrap the player combat HUD in `.elite-player-hud` to show the partner portrait. The attack renderer searched for a direct `.battle-bottom > .combat-hud` child, then read `sr.left` from a missing source rectangle. The turn had already committed its damage and combat log, leaving commands disabled after an exception. The lookup now finds the nested HUD; missing source rectangles safely anchor locally, also supporting alternate layouts.

All 63 authored animation sheets are rebuilt from the exact original generation files, matched by SHA-256 against committed provenance. Frames retain 192 × 192 source pixels instead of 64 × 64: nine times as many pixels per frame. The original 192-pixel phone and 256-pixel larger-screen display sizes are retained. This restores real shading, fine edges and fragments rather than magnifying the low-resolution runtime sheets. All 18 ordinary and four signature atlases are similarly restored from their original pre-normalization repository artwork, at 192 pixels per cell. Original source dimensions are at least 256 pixels per cell, so this is downsampling genuine source detail, not inventing detail through upscaling.

Animation duration is 35% longer (about 26% slower) across move effects. The battle contact clock, per-strike counter timing, sprite playback and damage-number delay use matching durations. Original Mercy artwork, display size and timing remain unchanged.

Regression coverage performs actual damaging Avalon Cleave and Iron Lance rounds in all eight leader portrait layouts, checking both player and enemy HP, round advancement, FX cleanup and enabled commands. Full and Reduced motion are both exercised with a strict completion deadline. Pixel-art browser checks verify the unchanged phone footprint and increased source resolution, all 63 sheets and motion overrides. The normal signed-release pipeline runs the full game suite before packaging.
