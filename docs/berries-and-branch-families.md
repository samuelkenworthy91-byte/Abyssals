# v0.90.40 · Berries, branch families and Mabel's pantry

Approved for gameplay and APK/PWA release on 26 October 2026. Includes the earlier staged illustrated bag and Grand Exchange lift patch.

- New stable creature IDs 505–514, move IDs 730–740. Ten selected original battle portraits, ten eight-frame directional follower sheets, and explicit family shiny palettes. Existing species IDs and saves are unchanged.
- Flaskling (505): Dark, half final awarded EXP regardless of ability swapping, only Feeble Pulse and Cower as natural moves, no codex teaching. Rare 4% base-form encounters in Dorelem Scrub, levels 5–8. Evolves with its selected item only at level 40+ inside a Hell/Inferno/Underworld/Nether region. The currently playable Living Water / mortal-world maps and the mirror threshold do not bypass the Hell gate. Failed use preserves the item. The multiplier ends upon evolution.
- Scruffox (510): weak Fighting base, rare 4% encounters on North Scrub Road at levels 8–12. Four item branches at level 30: Arthox (attack), Alfredox (defence), Ronnifox (bruiser) and Reggifox (support/special attack). Reggifox is Fighting/Psychic with Mind Lance, Aura Pulse, Calm Thought, Future Read and relay/support options. No reputation flag is required.
- All eight evolution items are renewable in Marasen's 4F Evolution Atelier. Evolved split forms remain excluded from wild encounters. Collection accessibility is regression checked.
- Twelve real consumable held Berries: Dewberry, Hearthberry, Renewal Berry, Clearberry, Emberberry, Frostberry, Wakeberry, Sparkberry, Antidote Berry, Focusberry, Mightberry and Mindberry. HP, status/confusion, PP and pinch-stat triggers use the same held-item inventory and consumption path. Existing Feast, Forager, Berry Hoard, Shared Pantry, Scavenger and Tithe Charge hooks are preserved and exercised.
- Eight broad held items: Power Wrap (physical damage), Mind Lens (special damage), Keen Lens (accuracy), Vanguard Vest (SpD / status restriction), Reprisal Helm (contact punishment), Last Thread (consumable full-HP survival), Clear Herb (consumable negative-stage clearing), Pressure Orb (damage / HP cost). Sold on the Exchange's Relic Gallery floor. Last Thread does not protect against residual damage and can be followed by a lethal second hit; Pressure Orb can defeat its holder outside a non-lethal floor.
- Original character NPC-BERRY-MABEL, Mabel Rowan, runs a homely Berry Pantry in Dorelem's upper-left stablehouse. The original household conversation remains. New portrait, sixteen-frame human walking sheet and eight illustrated furniture assets share warm honey-oak, sage cloth and colourful berry baskets. Collision follows the counter, shelves, crates, plants and tasting table, keeping the entrance and central aisle clear.
- Standalone item images are alpha-trimmed with consistent safe padding. Berry/equipment icons are new and optimized. The HTML review is regenerated with the new content and tighter crops; it stays outside runtime packaging.

Research reference: Pokémon's official held-item strategy article (https://www.pokemon.com/us/features/the-incredible-importance-of-items) was consulted for the role/trade-off families. Abyssals uses original item names, artwork, ability interactions and the values described in its own in-game text.

Validation covers all item paths and region/level gates, half EXP plus Warbond, actual early encounter tables, item coverage, Berry consumption/regrowth, equipment restrictions and survival, save/reload purchases, real evolution UI, pantry doorway/collision, phone artwork and original signing. Build only through the existing signed Android workflow; package only dist/.
