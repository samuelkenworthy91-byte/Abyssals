# Abyssals — Eight town leaders: portrait and battle-art direction (working branch)

## Canon and safety
These are the eight established characters. Do not rename them, replace them with unrelated fantasy characters, or change existing plot roles, outcomes or pronouns. Existing portrait IDs remain `LDR-01` to `LDR-08`; `LDR-05-battle` is Khalid's distinct established full-body continuation. Art should be **eight separate transparent-background figures**, not a single character sheet. Review the original portraits and character catalog before replacing a runtime asset. Keep illustrated 2D JRPG/pixel-adjacent painterly treatment, not glossy 3D, photo collage or generic Dawn Bloom infantry.

## Battle presentation
- One unique, larger full-body portrait in each leader's pre-battle reveal; dramatic but legible on portrait Android screens.
- Enlarged small portrait in battle HUD with a distinct school-colour accent; ordinary trainers keep existing compact art.
- Narrative and existing leader dialogues stay unchanged. Do not add unrelated character names or fabricated town names.
- Art assets remain WebP with true alpha, proper bounds and no magenta fringes; existing IDs are preserved so dialogue and rematches resolve.
- Use existing image references as inputs for redraw, not anonymous concept-board faces. A concept collage is not shippable game art.

## Team and combat contract
- Six fixed *level slots* and one assigned elemental type per leader; species and ace chosen from eligible fully evolved species for each save, stored permanently.
- No legendary or unique one-of-a-kind creatures in ordinary leader teams.
- Every opponent has a valid, actual Prodigy-growth stat, legal moves and a complementary team role. Rematches preserve families rather than rerolling.
- Leaders spend turns to switch, predict damage from only the player's active partner and visible moves, avoid exploitable matchups, use heal/setup/status tactically and choose suitable replacements. No hidden knowledge of the player's command.
- No Android APK/PWA build until the combined patch is approved.
