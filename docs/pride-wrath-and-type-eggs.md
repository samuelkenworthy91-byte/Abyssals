# Pride, Wrath and Type Eggs — v0.90.41

Vainglulus now wears a sweeping formal ink courtier silhouette, white cravat and violet-lined mantle. Wrathulus is a six-armed ink swordsman with oversized ember-edged weapons. Both visibly retain the original one-eyed Flaskling inside a chest flask. Slothulus and Gulomunc retain their approved bodies, with Flaskling now visible in the chest or glass belly. All four have matching eight-frame directional followers. Stable species IDs, stats, types and evolution requirements are unchanged.

Sixteen inventory icons were regenerated cleanly to remove neighbouring atlas fragments. Six new renewable held items are sold in the Grand Exchange Relic Gallery: Duelist Grip (Attack ×1.2), Scholar Bead (Special Attack ×1.2), Fleet Anklet (Speed ×1.15), Bastion Plate (Defence ×1.2), Shelter Clasp (direct damage ×0.8 at full HP, checked per hit), and Siphon Charm (heal 1/8 of actual move damage, once per move). Equipment does not consume itself. Siphon cannot revive a holder defeated by contact retaliation.

The fox family retains five unique signature ability names. The Fixer now prepares a nonstacking 30% boost to the next special attack after a successful status move. Physical attacks, misses, protection and immunity retain the preparation. Dealing special damage spends it after the complete move; switching clears it. No fox ability requires double battles or an active ally.

Parting Blow (741) is a shared Fighting physical move: 70 power, 100 accuracy, 20 PP. Arthox learns it on evolution; Fighting species may use its renewable 3,500-doubloon Codex. It deals damage before switching to a chosen living benched partner. It never passes stages or an effigy. Misses, protection, immunity and contact retaliation KO prevent switching. Trapping or no available bench still allows the attack. Switching and re-entering refresh Sovereign Charge. The ordinary relay and restorative relay retain their existing rules.

Eighteen illustrated egg assets support two separately clipped shell halves. The left half shows the fixed hatchling species' first type; the right shows its second type, or repeats the first for a single type. Pouch and hatch confirmation use the same renderer. Existing eggs update visually without modifying their species, seed, inheritance, shiny result, walking progress, parents or hatch record. A shared shell mask aligns the halves.

Production references, generated source atlases and prompt descriptions are in `art-library/reviews/pride-wrath-2026-10-26/`. Only selected optimized sprites are admitted to `dist/`. The self-contained picture gallery is rebuilt from live assets and includes the updated creature plans, ability, Parting Blow Codex, six new held items and eighteen egg designs.

Verification includes dedicated behavioral tests for pivot success and failure paths, stage isolation, Sovereign Charge re-entry, The Fixer in singles, all new equipment, and all 513 species' egg types. The phone test checks real dual-type egg creation, save/reload and hatching, actual pivot recipient selection and battle replacement, plus artwork loading. The release workflow runs the complete behavior suite, shiny raster audit, phone interaction suite, packaging and original-certificate verification before publication.
